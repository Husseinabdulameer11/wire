# The levels of the exercise page, in order: folder | name | who it is for
easy   | Easy   | Never seen a logic gate? Start here: one gate at a time, gates combined into small real problems, and your first buses.
medium | Medium | Adders and subtractors, choosing and encoding, working with numbers on buses, and your first flip-flops, registers and counters.
hard   | Hard   | Shifters and an ALU, sequence finders, LFSRs, PWM, debouncing, counters with every feature, state machines, and a UART transmitter.
